🔄 1. Sector Rotation Strategy (Follow Smart Money)

Institutions deploy capital into entire sectors. You can track this trend using our Date Dropdown history.

  • Check today's Top Performing Sectors (e.g., Nifty Metal or Nifty Realty).
  • Use the Date Dropdown to look back over the last 3-4 trading days.
  • Action Rule: If a sector stays in the "Top Performing" list for 3 consecutive days, it has strong institutional momentum. Focus only on buying the strongest stocks in this sector!

💰 2. Top Liquidity Leaders Strategy (Institutional Buyers)

Big players trade only where there is heavy turnover (Value in Crores) to avoid slippage.

  • Look at the Top Liquidity Leaders (Turnover) cards.
  • Action Rule: If a stock suddenly enters the Top 10 Turnover list with a gain of >4%, it indicates fresh institutional buying today.
  • Verify using history: if it has been appearing in the list for 2-3 consecutive days, it is under heavy accumulation. Buy the dips in this stock for high-probability momentum returns!

⚖️ 3. Market Breadth & Volatility Sentiment

Assess the market health before placing any aggressive trades.

  • Bullish Bias: If India VIX is falling/stable and Advances are double or triple the Declines (e.g., 2:1 ratio), market breadth is strong. Buy dips on intraday setups.
  • Bearish Bias: If VIX is rising (>15-18) and Declines outnumber Advances significantly, the market is weak. Avoid aggressive long positions; focus on short trades or cash.

📅 The 5-Minute Daily Trading Routine

Every evening at 6:30 PM (after data is fetched): 1. Check the general market bias (Advances vs. Declines & India VIX).
2. Identify the strongest 1-2 sectors that have been leading for the last 3 days.
3. Filter the Top Liquidity Leaders within those strong sectors.
4. Add these filtered stocks to your watchlist for next-day breakout or dip-buying setups!
