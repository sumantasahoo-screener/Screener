📖 How to Read This Dashboard? (Guide to understanding FII/DII data — click to expand) ▼
🟢 What is FII Net Flow?
Total buying minus total selling by Foreign Institutional Investors (FII/FPI) today. +Positive = Buying = Bullish signal. These are massive market movers — follow their direction.
🔵 What is DII Net Flow?
Flow from Domestic Institutional Investors (Mutual Funds, LIC, Insurance). DIIs usually act counter to FIIs — when FIIs sell, DIIs buy (providing market support).
⚖️ What does Combined Net indicate?
Total of FII + DII. If both are positive, there is strong buying. If both are negative, expect heavy selling — bearish.
🐘 What is a Bulk Deal?
When a single client buys/sells more than 0.5% of a company's total equity in a single day. Reported by NSE after market closes (EOD).
🧱 What is a Block Deal?
Occurs only during a special 8:45–9:00 AM pre-market window. Negotiated trades of ₹10 Cr+. Usually executed by Promoters or large FIIs/MFs. Very strong signal.
📈 How to take a trade?
BUY deal spotted: Stock is bullish — consider going long near support.
SELL deal spotted: Distribution happening — exercise caution, consider shorting near resistance.
Promoter BUY: Go long — extremely strong signal!
