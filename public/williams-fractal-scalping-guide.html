Fractal Pattern — Detail Mein

5-candle ka pattern jo price ka turning point batata hai

F
Bill Williams Fractal
5-candle reversal pattern indicator
Pattern

Fractal ek 5-candle ka group hota hai. Beech wali (3rd) candle hi key hoti hai. Agar beech wali candle ki High ya Low baaki sab se alag ho, to fractal form hota hai.

▲ Bullish Fractal (Buy signal)
Pattern example
C3 Low < C1 Low
C3 Low < C2 Low
C3 Low < C4 Low
C3 Low < C5 Low
→ Bullish Fractal confirmed
Entry: C3 ke high ke break pe long lo
▼ Bearish Fractal (Sell signal)
Pattern example
C3 High > C1 High
C3 High > C2 High
C3 High > C4 High
C3 High > C5 High
→ Bearish Fractal confirmed
Entry: C3 ke low ke break pe short lo
Fractal ke baare mein yaad rakho:
Fractal complete hone mein 5 candles lagte hain
Pehle 4 candles form hone ke baad hi pata chalta hai — jaldi mat karo
Fractal sirf direction batata hai, timing nahi
Isliye Williams %R aur EMA se timing confirm karni padti hai
2-min chart pe fractals zyada frequent aate hain
Sab fractals pe trade mat karo — sirf filtered ones pe
Williams %R — Detail Mein

0 se -100 ke beech oscillate karta hai — market ka momentum gauge

%R
Williams Percent Range
Larry Williams ne develop kiya — 1973
Period: 14
%R = (Highest High − Close) ÷ (Highest High − Lowest Low) × −100

Example: High=48500, Close=48200, Low=47800
%R = (48500−48200) ÷ (48500−47800) × −100
%R = 300 ÷ 700 × −100 = −42.8 (Neutral zone)

EMA Filter — Trend Confirm Karo

Yeh sabse important add-on hai — false signals 50% tak kam ho jaate hain

E
Exponential Moving Average (EMA)
EMA 9 (fast) + EMA 21 (slow)
Trend Filter

EMA price ka average hota hai, lekin recent candles ko zyada weight deta hai. Do EMAs milake trend direction clearly dikhate hain.

BUY ke liye EMA conditions:
EMA 9 upar hai EMA 21 se
Fast line slow line ke upar — uptrend confirm
Price EMA 9 ke upar hai
Price apne short-term average ke upar — bullish
EMA 9 ne EMA 21 ko upar cross kiya
Golden cross — strong buy momentum signal
SELL ke liye EMA conditions:
EMA 9 neeche hai EMA 21 se
Fast line slow line ke neeche — downtrend confirm
Price EMA 9 ke neeche hai
Price apne short-term average ke neeche — bearish
EMA 9 ne EMA 21 ko neeche cross kiya
Death cross — strong sell momentum signal
EMA 9 (Fast — zyada responsive)
EMA 21 (Slow — trend line)
Price
Important Rule:
Agar Fractal aur %R buy signal de rahe hain, lekin EMA downtrend mein hai — to TRADE MAT LO. EMA filter ki wajah se hi 2 fail trades bachaye ja sakte the.

Common Mistakes — Inse Bachao

Yeh galtiyan 80% traders karte hain — aap mat karo

01
Sirf ek indicator pe trade karna
Sirf Fractal ya sirf %R dekh ke trade lena — yeh sabse badi galti hai. Dono milne chahiye.
Fix: Sab 4 indicators confirm ho, tab hi entry lo
02
Stop Loss set nahi karna
Bina SL ke trade — ek badi loss sab profits kha jaati hai. Har trade pe SL mandatory hai.
Fix: Entry ke saath hi SL Dhan pe set karo — fractal level pe
03
Overtrading — har signal pe entry
2-min chart pe bahut zyada signals aate hain. Sab pe trade karne se loss badh jaata hai.
Fix: Sirf top-quality setups lo — quality over quantity
04
Opening 15 minutes mein trade karna
9:15–9:30 mein market settle nahi hua hota — fake moves bahut aate hain.
Fix: 9:30 ke baad hi pehli trade lo
05
Revenge trading — loss recover karne ki koshish
Loss ke baad turant next trade — emotionally driven, plan ke bagair.
Fix: 3 consecutive losses ke baad screen band karo aur break lo
