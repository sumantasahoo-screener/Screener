💡 FOVOLT TRADING STRATEGY

  • OPTIONS BUY ZONE (Vol < 30%): In stocks ki implied volatility kam hoti hai. Inme Options buying (Call/Put) karna sasta aur safe hota hai. Trend aane par achha profit mil sakta hai.
  • CAUTION ZONE (Vol 30% - 50%): Ye medium volatility wale stocks hain. Inme trade kar sakte hain lekin tight stop-loss ke sath, kyunki premium decay aur spikes ka risk hota hai.
  • AVOID BUYING (Vol > 50%): In stocks me options bohot mehenge hote hain (high IV). Inko buy karne par Volatility Crush ka bada risk hota hai. Option selling ke liye ye acche ho sakte hain.
  • INTRADAY PICKS (Vol 35% - 65%): Inme din ke dauran achha momentum (swing) milta hai. Ye intraday traders ke liye best hote hain jo chote time frame me trade karte hain.
