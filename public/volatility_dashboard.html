📖 Volatility Strategy Guide

✅ Options Buying Zone

Jab annualised volatility 30% se kam ho toh options sasti hoti hain. Ye best time hai calls/puts khareedne ka. Vol kam hai matlab market aage move kar sakta hai aur premium bhi reasonable hai.

Annualised Vol: < 30%

⚠️ Caution Zone

Vol 30%–50% ke beech ho toh options thodi mehgi hain. Buying karo toh tight stop loss raho. Agar vol suddenly spike kiya hai toh koi news/event expected ho sakta hai — wait karo ya chhoti position lo.

Annualised Vol: 30% – 50%

🚫 Avoid Buying Options

Vol 50% se zyada ho toh options bahut costly hain. Market already move kar chuka hai. Options khareedne par "Vol Crush" ka risk zyada — vol giri toh option ka value bhi girega chahe direction sahi ho.

Annualised Vol: > 50%

📈 Intraday Equity Guide

High Vol (>35%) stocks intraday ke liye best hain — inme zyada movement hoti hai matlab profit opportunity zyada. Sudden vol spike (aaj ki vol > kal ki vol by 20%+) — kuch hone wala hai, news ya event. Intraday momentum trade ke liye watch karo. Low vol (<20%) stocks avoid karo intraday mein — ye sideways rahenge, brokerage aur slippage mein paisa jayega.
