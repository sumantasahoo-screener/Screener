What
StockTrade kya hai?
NSE F&O ka real-time momentum scanner
Overview ▼
StockTrade ek Baseline Comparison Tool hai — yeh ek time point ka snapshot (baseline) save karta hai aur baad mein live NSE data se compare karta hai.
Jab bhi price ya OI mein significant change aata hai — tool automatically signal cards dikhata hai jo batate hain kya ho raha hai.
Yeh tool intraday trading ke liye best hai — market hours mein har 10 minute par automatically baseline save hoti hai.
Data Source
Live NSE F&O data — real-time during market hours (9:15 AM to 3:30 PM IST)
Auto Save
Baselines har 10 minute mein automatically save hoti hain — manually bhi set kar sakte ho
Signals
Long Build, Short Build, Short Unwind, Long Unwind, OI Wall, A/D Ratio
Best Use
Intraday momentum catch karna — pehle 30 min aur last 1 hour mein sabse effective

Concept
Baseline Kya Hai?
Ek time ka complete NSE snapshot — reference point
Concept ▼
Baseline = ek specific time par saare F&O stocks ka price + OI snapshot. Yeh reference point ban jaata hai.
Jab aap Compare karte ho — current live data is snapshot se compare hota hai. Difference = signal.
Multiple baselines save ho sakti hain — 8:53 AM, 9:15 AM, 10:00 AM — aap koi bhi select karke compare kar sakte ho.

4 Main OI Signals
Bullish
Long Build
Price ↑ + OI ↑ — Fresh buying entering
Bullish ▼
Condition
Price Change = Positive
OI Change = Positive (up)
Strength
Price +1% + OI +5% = Strong
Price +0.5% + OI +2% = Weak
What Happens in Market
New long positions open ho rahi hain. Bulls fresh capital laga rahe hain — confidence high hai.
Futures mein heavy buying ho rahi hai. Institutions accumulate kar rahe hain.
Strongest signal tab hota hai jab multiple stocks ek saath Long Build dikhaayein — sector-wide momentum.
Trading Action
Long Build stocks mein Bull Call Spread ya Bull Put Spread below support. Direct call buy tab karo jab price strong support ke upar ho aur OI jump 10%+ ho. Stop Loss: Baseline ka low price.
Bearish
Short Build
Price ↓ + OI ↑ — Fresh selling entering
Bearish ▼
Condition
Price Change = Negative
OI Change = Positive (up)
Strength
Price -1% + OI +5% = Strong
Price -0.3% + OI +2% = Weak
What Happens in Market
New short positions open ho rahi hain. Bears actively futures sell kar rahe hain.
Institutions distribution kar rahe hain — retail ko dete hue exit kar rahe hain.
Agar Nifty bhi Short Build mein hai + individual stock bhi = double confirmation = strong bearish trade.
Trading Action
Short Build stocks mein Bear Put Spread ya Bear Call Spread above resistance. Direct put buy tab karo jab OI jump 15%+ ho aur price key support tod de. Stop Loss: Baseline ka high price.
Reversal
Short Unwind
Price ↑ + OI ↓ — Shorts covering their positions
Caution ▼
Condition
Price Change = Positive
OI Change = Negative (down)
Nature
Weak bullish — sirf shorts cover ho rahe hain, fresh buying nahi. Rally sustainable nahi.
What Happens in Market
Jinhonn shorting ki thi woh loss mein hain — forced buyback ho rahi hai. Price upar ja raha hai lekin genuine bulls nahi aa rahe.
Yeh rally bhi ekdum ulti ho sakti hai — "Short Squeeze Rally" kehte hain ise.
Trading Action
Short Unwind mein aggressive long mat lo. Agar already long ho toh partial profit book karo. Short Unwind ke baad Long Build aaye tab full entry karo — confirm karo ki genuine buyers aa rahe hain.
Exit
Long Unwind
Price ↓ + OI ↓ — Longs exiting their positions
Bearish ▼
Condition
Price Change = Negative
OI Change = Negative (down)
Nature
Longs exit kar rahe hain — distribution. Price girta hai + OI bhi girta hai = selling pressure.
What Happens in Market
Jo log long position mein the woh exit kar rahe hain — price support tod sakti hai aage jaake.
Long Unwind = do NOT buy the dip. Yeh weakness ka sign hai, strength ka nahi.
Trading Action
Dip buying avoid karo Long Unwind stocks mein. Agar already long ho toh exit karo. Long Unwind ke baad Short Build aaye = strong bearish confirmation = put spread le sakte ho.
Special Signals
Wall
OI Wall Signal 🧱
Highest OI concentration — key support or resistance
Special ▼
OI Wall icon (🧱) dikhta hai jab kisi stock ka OI ek specific level par bahut zyada concentrated ho — yeh level strong support ya resistance ban jaata hai.
Price ke neeche OI Wall = Strong Support — put writers is level ko defend karenge.
Price ke upar OI Wall = Strong Resistance — call writers price ko neeche rakhenge.
How to Trade
OI Wall ke neeche Bull Put Spread sell karo — wall aapki protection hai. OI Wall ke upar Bear Call Spread sell karo — wall aapki ceiling hai. Combine OI Wall with Long Build/Short Build for highest confidence.

A/D Ratio 📶 — Advance/Decline
Kitne stocks up hain vs kitne down hain
Market Breadth ▼

Pro Tip: A/D Ratio dekho pehle. Agar 70%+ advancing hai toh koi bhi Long Build stock ek achha trade hai. Agar 30% se kam advancing hai toh sirf best Short Build stocks lo — market ke against mat jao.
Filter
Price Δ MIN Slider
Minimum price change filter — noise hatao
Filter ▼
Price Δ MIN slider = minimum price change threshold. Default = 0.20%. Iska matlab: sirf woh stocks dikhao jinki price 0.20% se zyada move hui ho.
Slider badhao (e.g. 0.5%) = sirf strong movers dikhte hain — less noise, cleaner signals.
Slider ghataao (e.g. 0.05%) = zyada stocks dikhenge — early signals catch hote hain but more noise.

P/D Badge
Premium (P) aur Discount (D) Badge
Futures price vs Spot price — market sentiment indicator
Guide ▼
P Badge — Premium 🟢
Futures > Spot price. Bullish sign — market expects stock to go up. Large premium = strong bullish conviction.
D Badge — Discount 🔴
Futures < Spot price. Bearish sign — market expects stock to fall. Large discount = strong bearish conviction.
Combination Rule: Long Build + P Badge = Double Bullish Confirmation ✅. Short Build + D Badge = Double Bearish Confirmation ✅. Inhi combinations mein high confidence trades milte hain.

Mistakes
Common Mistakes — Inhe Avoid Karo
Beginners ki galtiyan jo aapko nahi karni
Warning ▼
❌ Kya Mat Karo
Sirf price change dekh ke trade mat lo — OI change equally important hai. Price up + OI down = Short Unwind = weak signal.
Opening 5 min mein trade mat lo — 9:15-9:20 AM mein bahut volatility hoti hai, signals false ho sakte hain. 9:20 ke baad baseline set karo.
Single tool pe rely mat karo — StockTrade ke signal ko Bhav tool se confirm karo (PCR + Max Pain). Ek tool = incomplete picture.
A/D ratio ignore mat karo — 30% se kam advancing mein Long Build stock mein bhi entry risky hai. Market current ke against mat jao.
Har compare pe trade mat karo — Sirf strong signals lo. Price +0.2% + OI +1% = very weak signal. Skip karo.
Golden Rule: StockTrade aapko kaunsi stocks watch karni hain batata hai — trade directly yahan se nahi lo. Pehle stock identify karo, phir Option Chain ya Bhav tool se confirm karo, tab entry lo.
