📊 Timeframe Alignment

Check 9:20, 9:30, 9:45, and 10:15 timeframes. Single signals are risky; consistency across majority timeframes confirms strength.

⚡ 15M Trend Filter

The 15M Trend is your primary filter. BULL signals require an UP trend; BEAR signals require a DOWN trend.

⚠️ Entry Protocol

If the ORB signal is BULL but the 15M Trend is DOWN (or vice versa), DO NOT ENTER. This is a high-probability trap zone.
