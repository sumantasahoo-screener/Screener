What
OptionTrade kya hai?
Option OI ke zariye bullish/bearish stocks ek nazar mein
Overview▼
OptionTrade ek Option OI Scanner hai — yeh NSE ke saare F&O stocks ki Call OI vs Put OI compare karta hai aur bullish/bearish signal deta hai.
StockTrade (futures OI) se alag — OptionTrade option chain ka data use karta hai. Dono saath mein use karo tab sabse powerful signal milta hai.
Tool mein 2 modes hain — Live Data (abhi ka snapshot) aur Day View (poore din ke snapshots ka trend).
Data Source
NSE Option Chain — real-time Call OI aur Put OI har strike pe
Core Logic
PCR (Put-Call Ratio) + CE Wall + PE Wall + OI buildup = combined signal
Live Mode
Abhi ka snapshot — ek click mein saare stocks ka option sentiment
Day View Mode
Subah se abhi tak ki snapshots — trend dekho, momentum pakdo
Diff

Dono alag data sources, saath mein use karo
Concept▼

Power Combo: StockTrade Long Build + OptionTrade Bullish PCR = Double confirmation = Highest confidence bullish trade. Dono ek saath agree karein tab hi full size trade lo.

RELIANCE — Spot: 2840. CE Wall: 2900 (CE Dist: +2.1%). PE Wall: 2800 (PE Dist: -1.4%).
PCR: 1.45 (Bullish). PE Wall 2800 is already below spot — broken = strong support zone.
Signal: Bullish. CE Wall 2900 is target. Bull Put Spread below 2800 PE Wall is the trade.

Wall
CE Wall aur PE Wall — Detailed Guide
Support, resistance, trap aur breakout signals
Guide▼
CE Wall — Resistance
Highest Call OI strike. Call writers yahan defend karte hain. Price ke upar hai toh resistance. Spot se zyada door = more room to rally.
PE Wall — Support
Highest Put OI strike. Put writers yahan defend karte hain. Price ke neeche hai toh support. Spot ke bahut paas = strong floor.
CE Dist%MeaningSignalAction
Negative (e.g. -1%)Spot already above CE WallBullish breakout! CE Wall broken.Buy calls or Bull Call Spread
0% to +1%Spot approaching CE WallWatch for breakout or rejectionWait for confirmation
+2% to +5%CE Wall safely aboveNormal — room to rallyBull Put Spread below support
PE NegativeSpot below PE WallBearish breakdown! Support broken.Buy puts or Bear Call Spread
Wall Shift Alert: Agar CE Wall ya PE Wall strike change ho jaaye (e.g. CE Wall 22000 se 22200 ho gayi) toh yeh bahut important signal hai — option writers positions shift kar rahe hain. Naya level naya resistance/support ban jaata hai.
What
Day View kya hai?
Poore din ke snapshots ka timeline — trend dekho
Concept▼
Day View = poore din mein baar baar liye gaye snapshots ka collection. Yeh "trend" dikhata hai — sirf ek point ka nahi, din bhar ki direction ka.
Ek snapshot = ek moment. Day View = multiple moments = trend line. PCR 9:20 AM pe 1.1 tha, 11:00 AM pe 1.4 tha, 1:00 PM pe 0.9 hai — yeh trend batata hai ki sentiment bearish ho raha hai.
Sahi trade tab milta hai jab trend direction clear ho — Day View isi ke liye hai.

2

3

4
PCR trend dekho
Agar PCR consistently badhta raha = bullish momentum building. PCR girta raha = bearish pressure badh raha hai. Yeh trend hi real signal hai.
Patterns
Day View Patterns — Kya Dekha Toh Kya Karo
PCR trend patterns aur unka trading matlab
Guide▼
Pattern 1
Steadily Rising PCR — 🟢 Strong Bullish
9:20 AM: 0.9 → 11:00 AM: 1.1 → 1:00 PM: 1.4 → 2:30 PM: 1.7. Market consistently bullish ho raha hai. Put writers protect kar rahe hain. Action: Full size bull trade lo.
Pattern 2
Steadily Falling PCR — 🔴 Strong Bearish
9:20 AM: 1.4 → 11:00 AM: 1.1 → 1:00 PM: 0.8 → 2:30 PM: 0.6. Sentiment deteriorate ho raha hai. Call writers dominant ho rahe hain. Action: Bear trade lo, longs avoid karo.
Pattern 3
PCR Oscillating — ↔ Sideways/Confused
9:20 AM: 1.1 → 11:00 AM: 0.9 → 1:00 PM: 1.2 → 2:30 PM: 0.8. No clear trend — market confused hai. Action: Iron Condor ya no trade. Wait for clarity.
Pattern 4
Sudden PCR Spike — ⚠️ Reversal Alert
Morning 0.8 (bearish) → Afternoon suddenly 1.6+. Short covering ya panic put buying ho raha hai. Market reversal possible. Action: Close bear trades, watch for upside reversal.

Bullish
Strong Bullish Signals — Kya Dekha Toh Buy?
Yeh combination dikhey toh bullish trade lo
Setup▼
✅ Ideal Bullish Setup
PCR above 1.3 — strong put support below
PE Wall is below spot price aur intact hai — solid floor
CE Wall far above spot (2%+) — room to rally
Day View mein PCR rising trend — bullish momentum building
StockTrade mein bhi Long Build dikh raha ho — double confirmation
Trade Karo
Bull Put Spread: PE Wall se 1-2% neeche sell karo. Bull Call Spread: ATM call buy + CE Wall ke paas call sell. Direct Call Buy: Sirf tab jab score 80+ ho aur trend clear ho.
Bearish
Strong Bearish Signals — Kya Dekha Toh Sell?
Yeh combination dikhey toh bearish trade lo
Setup▼
✅ Ideal Bearish Setup
PCR below 0.8 — call writers dominant, resistance strong above
CE Wall is just above spot (ya broken neeche aaya) — ceiling tight
PE Wall broken neeche — support nahi raha
Day View mein PCR consistently gir raha hai
StockTrade mein Short Build aur D Badge dikh raha ho — double confirmation
Trade Karo
Bear Call Spread: CE Wall ke upar sell karo. Bear Put Spread: ATM put buy + PE Wall ke neeche put sell. Direct Put Buy: Sirf tab jab score bearish 80+ ho aur trend clear ho.
Neutral
Neutral Signals — Kab Range Mein Trade Karein?
PCR 0.8-1.2 + balanced walls = sideways setup
Setup▼
Neutral Setup
PCR between 0.8 to 1.2 — no clear directional bias
CE Wall aur PE Wall dono almost equal distance from spot
Day View mein PCR oscillating — no clear trend
Trade Karo
Iron Condor: CE Wall ke upar Bear Call Spread + PE Wall ke neeche Bull Put Spread. Dono sides se premium collect karo. Iron Butterfly: Agar PCR exactly 1.0 ke paas ho toh ATM straddle sell karo with wings. Neutral mein directional trade = low probability.

Mistakes
Common Mistakes — Avoid Karo
OptionTrade use karte waqt yeh galtiyan mat karo
Warning▼
❌ Kya Mat Karo
Sirf PCR dekh ke trade mat lo — PCR ek indicator hai, complete signal nahi. CE Wall, PE Wall, aur StockTrade se confirm karo.
Extreme PCR values mein aggressive mat jao — PCR 1.8+ ya 0.4 se kam = reversal zone. Size chhota rakho.
Single snapshot pe depend mat karo — Day View trend zyada reliable hai. Ek reading alag ho sakti hai, trend nahi.
Wall shifts ignore mat karo — Agar CE Wall ya PE Wall shift hua toh immediately apni position reassess karo.
Index aur stock ek saath mat trade karo — Nifty ka PCR alag hota hai, RELIANCE ka alag. Dono alag-alag analyze karo.
Golden Rule: OptionTrade batata hai option market kahan positioned hai — yeh predict nahi karta price kahan jaayegi. Option positioning strong support/resistance create karta hai, toot bhi sakta hai. Hamesha stop loss rakho.
